## Prompt

A 45-second vertical (9:16) claymation story for children aged 3 to 5. At a golden-hour lily pond, Lulu the lime-green frog and Bo the orange-shelled turtle both want to ride a pink petal slide into the water. They race to the top of the rock, shout "Me first!", bump, and tumble back onto the moss. Bo has an idea: "Let's take turns!" Lulu slides first with a big splash, then Bo slides on his shell. A yellow clay star floats over whoever has the turn. The song asks "Whose turn is it now?" and pauses so children can answer. They end side by side on a lily pad, waving goodbye. Swinging kalimba and steel-drum song, warm female voice. Shots: crane down, side tracking, low water-level angle, close-ups, overhead, over-the-shoulder, ride-along down the slide, push-in, crane up.
