## Prompt

A 48-second claymation fable for children aged 3 to 5. In a sunny picnic corner of the meadow, Pip sits in the middle of
a giant pink flower and calls "Help!" with a cheeky grin. Tumble races from the picnic blanket to save him, trips at a
puddle and lands in it with a splash. "Just joking!" giggles Pip. Pip calls again; this time Tumble leaps the puddle,
slides right past the flower and spins to a dizzy stop. "Oh, Pip, not another joke!" Pip flutters over to the picnic
basket to peek at the berries, hops in, and the tall open lid wobbles and flops shut on top of him. "Help! It's really
true this time!" Tumble stops: is it a joke? He decides to check anyway, lifts the lid, and Pip pops out, sorry and a
bit wobbly. "Sorry, Tumble!" They hug. On Tumble's paw Pip sings the moral, they do the series dance, and in the button
Pip sits on the closed lid and they wave bye-bye.
Gentle storybook folk at 100 BPM in D major with acoustic guitar, glockenspiel, bassoon for Tumble and tin whistle for Pip.

## How it differs from the rest of the batch

- **Set corner:** the picnic corner (blanket, basket, a giant flower, a puddle) with the cottage in the background.
  Fable 1 used the stump and the stream, Fable 2 the apple tree, the stump and a leaf pile. No stump, no tree in the
  action.
- **Gag:** a belly-flop splash and a skid-and-spin past the flower (Fable 1: wrapped in string; Fable 2: dive into the
  leaf pile). The trouble prop (a lid that flops shut) is new.
- **Story shape:** the same call happens three times, and the third time is real. Pip, not Tumble, makes the mistake
  and says sorry. Tumble's choice ("check anyway") is the turn.
- **Camera plan:** opens on a macro of Pip (Fables 1 and 2 open on Tumble), a high wide title, a ground-level splash and
  a low-angle basket insert.

## Softening (ages 3 to 5)

No wolf, no danger, nobody hurt or scared. The puddle is shallow and Tumble laughs it off; the spin is silly, not a
fall. Inside the basket Pip is cosy, just stuck: the lid is light and the basket only wobbles. Tumble never scolds; he
says "not another joke" gently and still checks. Pip says sorry and they hug before the moral.
