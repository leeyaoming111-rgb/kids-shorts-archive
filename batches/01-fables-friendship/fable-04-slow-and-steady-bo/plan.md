# Tumble & Pip Fables 4: Slow and Steady Bo

- **job:** batch 01, fable 04 (`fable-04-slow-and-steady-bo`), built on the shared kit (`common/fablekit.py`)
- **status:** plan written; animatic in progress.
- **source:** Aesop's fable *The Tortoise and the Hare* (public domain), retold with our cast
- **lesson (SEL, social-emotional learning):** keep going step by step; don't show off; be a good sport when a friend wins
- **guest:** Bo the turtle from the archive one-off "Lulu & Bo" (mint face, orange shell), his clay model ported
  unchanged into this fable's `code/meshes.py`, with his own cast voice for spoken gags (Voice Design)
- **length:** 48.0 s (20 bars at 100 BPM). Episode cut: 41.2 s

## Prompt

A 48-second claymation fable for children aged 3 to 5:
- On a sunny morning in the Picnic Meadow, Tumble the fox cub and Bo the turtle stand at a chalk start line. Pip, the
  referee, perches on a pebble with a tiny chequered flag: "Ready, steady... go, go, go!" The finish is the big stump.
- Tumble zooms off in a blur ("Zoom, zoom! I'm the fastest fox!") and skids to a stop past the giant mushrooms.
  Bo plods on, step by step, smiling: "Step by step, I'm slow but sure."
- Tumble looks back at tiny far-away Bo, shows off ("Too easy! Time for a tiny rest!"), yawns and lies down in the
  shade of a giant mushroom. Every snore puffs the mushroom cap up and down.
- Bo plods past the snoring fox. From the stump, Pip waves the flag: "Wake up, Tumble! Look at Bo!"
- Tumble jolts awake, his legs spin in the air like a cartoon, and he zooms off, but Bo's nose touches the stump
  just before he skids in. Tumble is a good sport: "Well done, Bo!"
- Bo sings the moral, "Slow and steady gets there too!", with Pip on Tumble's paw. The series dance follows, with Bo
  swaying beside them, and at the end everyone says "Bye-bye!", Bo a beat later and very slowly.

## Differences from the other fables in the batch

- New corner of the meadow: a race course on a diagonal across open grass from a chalk start line to the big stump
  by the stream, past two giant mushrooms. No apple tree (Fable 2), no kite (Fable 1).
- First guest character (Bo) and a three-character staging; the moral is sung by the guest.
- Gag shapes: a snore that bobs a mushroom cap, and cartoon spinning legs. Fable 1 had a wrap-and-spin, Fable 2 a dive
  into a leaf pile.
- Pip is a referee with a flag, not a helper: the fix is Tumble being a good sport.
- Camera plan: the first ground-level tracking shot, the first over-the-shoulder looking back down a long course.
